Helpful Seasonal Farming Tips

Quick, actionable insights tested by Indian agricultural research stations for higher productivity.

Kharif Season Advisory (खरीप हंगाम)

Always perform seed treatment with Trichoderma or Rhizobium before sowing soybean, cotton, or groundnut to prevent collar rot and wilt.

Rabi Crop Nutrition (रब्बी हंगाम)

Apply nitrogen in 2-3 split doses for wheat and gram. Ensure the first irrigation at the Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage (21 days).

Summer & Drip Irrigation (उन्हाळी पिके)

Use organic mulching or plastic mulch in vegetable plots during summer to reduce soil moisture evaporation by up to 40%.
